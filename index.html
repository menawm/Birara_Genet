The Problem

Skills training is rare. Real apprenticeships are rarer.

In Addis Ababa, thousands of young adults finish school with no clear path to work. Without access to practical skills and employer connections, talented people remain unemployed — not because they lack potential, but because no one opened the door.

Birara Genet exists to close that gap with serious, hands-on programs that lead to real jobs.

Who We Are

Built on the belief that every person deserves a chance.

Since 2013, Birara Genet has been opening doors for people who have been left behind by the formal job market.

Our Mission

Skills, connections, and stability — for everyone.

Birara Genet is a Charitable Organization based in Bole Sub-City, Addis Ababa. We partner with training institutions and employers to create vocational pathways for individuals who would otherwise have no access to formal employment. We train people. We place them. We follow up. We don't stop until they have stable income.

Our Programs

Two pathways to real careers.

Hands-on, employer-connected training programs designed for people who are ready to work.

Vocational Training Program

Our core vocational training program equips participants with practical, marketable skills in trades and services. Training is delivered in partnership with accredited institutions and designed around real employer needs in Addis Ababa.

Practical skills Employer-connected Certificate upon completion

Who it's for: Adults in Addis Ababa seeking stable employment

Outcome: Job placement support and ongoing follow-up

Grad School Program

A specialized pathway for graduates who have completed formal education but need practical, employable skills to enter the workforce. Bridges the gap between academic knowledge and real-world application.

Graduate-focused Skills bridging Professional development

Who it's for: University and college graduates seeking employment

Outcome: Career placement in relevant fields
